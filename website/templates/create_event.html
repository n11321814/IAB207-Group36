Acknowledgement of Country

What's the difference? A Welcome to Country can only be delivered by a Traditional Owner or Elder of that specific land. An Acknowledgement of Country can be given by anyone, and is a statement of respect for the Traditional Custodians of the land an event takes place on.

Avoid common mistakes: a generic, copy-pasted line with no connection to the actual venue is the most common criticism of Acknowledgements. Naming the correct Traditional Owner group, and using "Traditional Owners/Custodians" rather than "landowners," are both simple ways to make yours genuine rather than tokenistic.

Not sure which group is connected to your venue's land? Check the AIATSIS Map of Indigenous Australia before writing an Enhanced Acknowledgement below.
